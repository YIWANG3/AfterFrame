AfterFrame 0.5.11 brings LUTs to the editor on the Mac: see a photo through every `.cube` LUT you have, and apply one at the strength you want. Edited photos are also saved at a higher JPEG quality, and a photo put back into a watched folder no longer shows as missing.

## Download

| | File | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| **Mac** | `AfterFrame-0.5.11-arm64.dmg` | macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon |
| **Windows** | `AfterFrame-0.5.11-x64-setup.exe` | Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit |

The Windows installer isn't signed yet. When Windows says "Windows protected your PC" (Windows 已保护你的电脑), click **More info** (更多信息), then **Run anyway** (仍要运行). To update, run the new installer: your catalogs and settings stay. LUTs, like Stickers, People and Scene Depth, are in the macOS app only for now.

## Highlights

### LUTs

The editor has a **LUT** tool, second in its tool rail, that applies `.cube` LUTs to a photo.

- **The photo through every LUT:** a grid of previews, grouped by pack and folder, with search. Click one to grade the photo, or step through them with the arrow keys.
- **Strength** from 0 to 100 %, and **Hold to see the original**. The saved photo is graded at full size, after the crop and before margins, frames and text.
- **Your LUT library:** **Add LUTs** either imports LUT files, copied into AfterFrame's LUT library with duplicates skipped, or adds a LUT folder, read where it is with nothing copied. LUTs you put into either folder in Finder show up when you come back to the app. Settings ▸ Library ▸ LUTs shows the library's size and the folders it reads.
- **Favorites** and the LUTs you used recently come first. Filters show only favorites, or hide Log LUTs.
- **Log LUTs**, made for S-Log3, F-Log, LogC and other Log video footage, are tagged Log from their name, and you can change the tag. On a photo they usually come out dark and oversaturated.
- **RAW files** are graded on Apple's rendering of the RAW, not the camera's embedded JPEG, which has the camera maker's look baked in. The first time you open the tool on a RAW, rendering it takes a second or two, a few seconds for a 100 MP file.

The LUT tool reads 3D `.cube` LUTs. 1D LUTs aren't supported yet.

### Also in this release

- **Edited photos are saved at JPEG quality 92 instead of 80.** This was the case for any save with a text or sticker layer, a border or a wash, and after Apply.
- **The editor shows the photo while it loads:** the catalog's preview, dimmed, under a spinner. The Save button spins while saving.
- **Watched folders:** a photo moved out of a watched folder and put back, for example with Put Back from the Trash, no longer keeps its "Original file moved or deleted" badge until you refresh.
- **Collage:** the Add Images and Replace Image pickers no longer shake when the photos almost fill the window.
