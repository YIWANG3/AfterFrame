This release makes AfterFrame work on macOS 14 again. It also fixes what turned up using 0.5.8 on real libraries: imports that locked the rest of the app out of the catalog, RAW compare and full-size edits, and thumbnails that blinked.

## Highlights

### macOS 14 and later

- **0.5.8 couldn't import or browse on macOS 14 or older.** The window opened, but the background process that imports and reads photos was built for macOS 15 and couldn't start. 0.5.9 builds it for macOS 14, and the build now checks every bundled file against that.
- **macOS 12 and 13:** macOS says the app needs a newer system, instead of opening an app that can't load photos. Every Apple Silicon Mac can update to macOS 14, and People, stickers and depth needed it already.

### The app stays usable during an import

- **While an import runs,** the photo count, the gallery, job progress, ratings and folder changes keep working. Before, the count could drop to 0, the gallery could fail to load, and changes could fail with "database is locked".
- **Mixed folders on slow drives:** importing RAW, TIFF and JPEG from a hard drive no longer stops partway with "database is locked".
- **Cancel** takes effect sooner.

### RAW

- **Compare** shows both RAW files. Before, it showed two broken images.
- **Full-size edits:** when a RAW's embedded preview is smaller than the photo, the editor now edits and saves at the RAW's full size. A Fujifilm GFX RAF saved at 4000×3000; it now saves at 11648×8736. The editor waits for the full-size render the first time. RAWs that embed a full-size JPEG (Canon and most others) are unchanged. For the others the picture comes from the RAW itself, so the camera's look, such as a film simulation, isn't kept.
- **Edits stay linked to their RAW:** what the editor, Split and AI Repaint save from a RAW links back to it. The Inspector's RAW source row opens the RAW in the gallery.
- **Two copies of the same RAW** are two photos. Before, they became one and their cards blinked every couple of seconds.

### Gallery and map

- **Thumbnails stop blinking** when photos are added while you scroll, for example by a watched folder. Some photos showed twice in a row and blinked.
- **Deleting or removing photos** no longer makes the next page of the gallery skip as many.
- **The Inspector follows the open photo** when its file is moved, put back or relinked. Before, it kept the old path until you selected another photo.
- **Smart collections:** a photo rated below the rule leaves the grid at once.
- **Going somewhere and straight back** no longer shows the place you left.
- **Toolbar blur:** what scrolls under the toolbar is blurred again, not just dimmed.
- **Map cluster thumbnails** stay put while you drag the map.
- **The map's viewport filter** lists only photos with a marker in view. A city-level place guess used to match an area about 30 km wide, so a view over the next town listed that city's photos.
- **Discover on the map:** Show Map from a memory keeps its place, opens framed on it, and puts the place back when you close the map.

### Folders

- **New folders go on top** of the list, and the list scrolls to them.
- **Sort** the folder list by custom order, name, newest or most photos, and **search** it by name.

### Also in this release

- **Imported and Modified times** in the Inspector are in your time zone. They were off by your offset from UTC, 7 hours in California. Capture times still follow the camera's clock.
- **In Chinese,** background jobs name their steps in Chinese, and Discover memories show their dates as 2024年1月2日–6日.
- **The sample library** shows up at once, and new catalogs open faster.
- **The sticker library** is saved in one step, so the Stickers panel can't briefly show none and a crash can't lose it.
