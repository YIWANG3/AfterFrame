AfterFrame 0.5.13 finds the photos that look alike, gives every folder a page of its own, and makes collages blend, measure and export larger.

## Download

| | File | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| **Mac, Apple Silicon** | `AfterFrame-0.5.13-arm64.dmg` | macOS 14 or later |
| **Mac, Apple Silicon, older macOS** | `AfterFrame-0.5.13-macOS12-arm64.dmg` | macOS 12 or 13 |
| **Mac, Intel** | `AfterFrame-0.5.13-macOS12-Intel.dmg` | macOS 12 or later |
| **Windows** | `AfterFrame-0.5.13-x64-setup.exe` | Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit |

The two macOS 12 builds are the same app without People, Scene Depth and Stickers, which use Apple's on-device models that need macOS 14. On an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 14 or later, take the first one. The Intel build reads RAW files with an older LibRaw (0.21), which can't decode DNGs compressed with JPEG XL.

The Windows installer isn't signed yet. When Windows says "Windows protected your PC" (Windows 已保护你的电脑), click **More info** (更多信息), then **Run anyway** (仍要运行). To update, run the new installer: your catalogs and settings stay. LUTs, Stickers, People and Scene Depth are in the macOS app only for now.

**Catalog upgrade:** 0.5.13 adds a look-alike index to a catalog the first time it opens it. After that, 0.5.12 and older can't open that catalog. The first open also starts a one-time **Look-alikes** job that measures the photos already in the library; it shows in the Activity Center like any other job.

## Highlights

### Photos that look alike

- Every photo now gets a perceptual hash and, on the Mac, Apple Vision's image feature print, both taken from its preview. Two photos that are another export of one picture, a re-encode or a crop land close together; two different shots of one scene don't.
- **Look-alikes** (近似重复) appears in the sidebar as soon as the library has any: every photo that has a look-alike, in one view, so you can decide which to keep.
- **Find Similar** (查找相似照片) in a photo's right-click menu opens the photos that look like it, nearest first. Search and filters still narrow that view.
- The Inspector shows a **Similar** strip of up to eight.
- Photos and RAW files are compared within their own kind. On Windows the comparison uses the hash alone for now.

### A page for your folders

- Click the folder icon in the sidebar's folder header, or **All folders** on Discover's Albums row, to see every folder at once: a mosaic of its newest four photos, its name, its photo count and the span of capture dates.
- Sort by name, newest, most photos, your own order, last added to or capture date. Filter by status (empty, changed in the last 30 days), photo count, capture dates or when a photo was last added. Search by name.
- A list view sorts by its column headers. Double-click or press Enter to open a folder in the gallery; rename inline.
- Select folders with click, ⌘-click, Shift and ⌘A, then annotate or delete the selection with one confirmation. The photos stay in the library.
- The folder page keeps its own sort and view; the sidebar keeps its own.

### Collage

- **Exports are 4096 px wide by default**, up from 3000. A width you set before is kept.
- **Edge blend**: a fourth canvas slider cross-fades neighbouring photos into each other across a band as wide as you choose. The page's outer edges stay hard, and corner radius doesn't apply while blending. Works in the batch too and for an agent's `render_collage`.
- **Highlight uncovered areas** hatches the parts of a cell its photo no longer reaches, after zooming out or panning off an edge, and counts the cells. Display only, the export is untouched.
- **Gap, padding, corner radius and edge blend take a typed number** beside each slider, and the arrow keys step the field instead of nudging the selected photo.
- **7, 8 and 9 photos get a single-row layout**, as 2 to 6 already had.

### Copy puts the files on the clipboard

⌘C on selected photos (Ctrl+C on Windows), Edit › Copy, or the gallery's new right-click **Copy** item puts the files on the clipboard the way Finder's ⌘C does: ⌘V in Finder makes copies, and pasting into WeChat, Notes or anything that takes a file drop gets the photo. In a text field, ⌘C still copies text; **Copy File Path** and **Copy Name** are unchanged.

### Also in this release

- **The folder view shows the face badge again.** It had been left out of that view's data.
- **A batch collage export says when pages failed** instead of reporting success, and saves to the right path on Windows.
- **A HEVC clip waits for its playback copy in silence.** On Windows, while the H.264 copy was made, the original could play its sound with no picture and run the scrubber to the end.
- **Notifications show their whole message**, up to three lines, instead of being cut to one.
- **Chinese throughout:** the crop tool's Free and Original (自由 / 原始比例), the editor's "Applied", "Browse failed", the Activity Center's queued-changes line, the job-failed notification and the annotation notifications were English in the Chinese interface.
- **Esc closes the editor from a focused slider**, such as LUT strength or zoom.
- **A transcode that stalls on the Mac now times out** instead of leaving the video waiting forever.
- **People on Intel Macs:** importing a custom face model failed its check with "invalid JSON" because Apple's Metal framework printed a warning into the worker's output. The worker now keeps that output for its own protocol.
- **A cached depth map is read at its real size.** The cache assumed every model produced 518 × 392.
- **MCP:** three new tools, `find_similar`, `list_lookalikes` and `index_lookalikes`; `search_assets` takes `similar_to`; `manage_collections` lists each folder's cover previews, capture span and last-added time; 37 tools in all. The server reports the app's version instead of 0.1.0.
