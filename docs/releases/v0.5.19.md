AfterFrame 0.5.19 is about keeping a growing library in order: folders nest, tags sit in groups, the sidebar folds, and a pasted list of file numbers finds its photos. Opening a catalog in this version upgrades it, and older versions can't open it afterwards (see below).

## Download

| | File | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| **Mac, Apple Silicon** | `AfterFrame-0.5.19-arm64.dmg` | macOS 14 or later |
| **Mac, Apple Silicon, older macOS** | `AfterFrame-0.5.19-macOS12-arm64.dmg` | macOS 12 or 13 |
| **Mac, Intel** | `AfterFrame-0.5.19-macOS12-Intel.dmg` | macOS 12 or later |
| **Windows** | `AfterFrame-0.5.19-x64-setup.exe` | Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit |

The two macOS 12 builds are the same app without People, Scene Depth and Stickers, which use Apple's on-device models that need macOS 14. On an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 14 or later, take the first one. The Intel build reads RAW files with an older LibRaw (0.21), which can't decode DNGs compressed with JPEG XL.

The Windows installer isn't signed yet. When Windows says "Windows protected your PC" (Windows 已保护你的电脑), click **More info** (更多信息), then **Run anyway** (仍要运行). To update, run the new installer: your catalogs and settings stay. LUTs, Stickers, People and Scene Depth are in the macOS app only for now.

**This version upgrades your catalog.** The first time 0.5.19 opens a catalog it adds the folder tree and the tag groups, and 0.5.18 or older can't open that catalog afterwards. A copy of the catalog as it was is left next to it (`catalog.sqlite3.schema12.bak`), should you need to go back.

## New

### Folders nest

Flat folders stop working once there are dozens of trips, clients or years. Folders now go inside folders, any depth.

- **The sidebar is a tree.** Chevrons open and close a folder (←/→ on the keyboard), and what is open is remembered per catalog. A row's hover button makes a subfolder. Drag a folder onto the middle of another to nest it; its top and bottom edges still reorder within a level.
- **A parent shows everything under it**: its own photos plus those of every folder below, each once. A toolbar switch, there only when the folder has subfolders, turns that to this folder only, and the choice is remembered. The title shows the path (`Trips / Japan`).
- **The folder page** shows where a nested folder sits, and its right-click menu has **New subfolder** and **Move to…**. Deleting a folder takes its subfolders with it, and the confirmation says so.
- **Every folder picker reaches every level**: the right-click **Add to Folder** list, the collage source and the filter bar's folder chip.
- A deep tree keeps its names on screen: the indent tightens as the tree gets deeper.

Smart collections keep their own sidebar section and don't nest.

### Tags by group

Tags were one flat pool where the AI's and your own mixed, `sunset` and `日落` were two tags, and nothing could be renamed or merged. Now every tag belongs to a group, or to none, and a tag can have aliases that resolve to it.

- **Thirteen built-in groups**: weather, season, time of day, light, subject, place, occasion, gear, composition, style, color, mood and use. Make your own next to them.
- **One tag, both languages.** A built-in vocabulary of about 230 concepts knows both spellings, so `sunset` and `日落` are the same tag, shown in the language the interface is in. On upgrade, existing tags are merged and grouped this way.
- **The inspector** shows a photo's tags by group. A tag's menu filters by it, moves it to another group, pins, renames or removes it. Pinned tags are one click away when adding.
- **The filter bar's Tags** is a grouped list you can search across groups and aliases. Tick a group's heading for "any tag of this group", and choose how tags match: any, one per group, all, or none.
- **A Tags page** in the sidebar lists every tag by group with its count, aliases and pin. Rename, merge, move, pin and delete tags there, make and delete groups, and have the AI fill a group.
- **Remove Tags…** (**Shift+T**) takes tags off the selected photos, next to **Add Tags…** (**T**).
- Typing in the search box offers matching tags; one click turns the word into a tag filter.

### Find by List

A list of file numbers arrives in a message (`1723, 1746 1754`) and the photos have to be found. The new button at the right end of the search box takes the list as it is.

- **Paste it in whatever shape.** Anything that can be part of a camera's file name is kept and everything else separates, so commas, bars, line breaks and whole sentences all work. `B0001723.3FR`, `DSC_0004` and plain `1723` are all read; "12 photos" and "14:32" are not taken for numbers.
- **Each entry says what it found**: one photo, several (hover for the names), none, or too many to be a pick. Click an entry to leave it out.
- **A photo's own name or its RAW's.** The number of the RAW finds the JPEG it is paired with.
- **Then filter or file.** **Filter** shows exactly those photos in the grid, as a filter you can open again or save as a smart collection. **Add** puts them into a new or an existing folder; inside a folder, the new one is made under it.

The list is looked up in the place you are in: all photos, a smart collection, or a folder.

### A sidebar that folds

- The **Smart Collections** and **Folders** sections fold down to their heading, each remembered. A folded section that holds the place you are in shows a dot.
- With many smart collections, the section scrolls within itself and no longer pushes the folders out of the sidebar.
- **Has RAW** left the sidebar's fixed list and is now a filter: photos with a paired RAW, or without one.

## For agents (MCP)

38 tools, up from 37.

- **`manage_tags`** (new): groups, aliases, rename, merge, move, pin and delete. `list_tags` answers by group; `search_assets` takes `tag_group` and `tag_match: by_group`; `update_assets` takes `tag_group`.
- **Folders**: `manage_collections` `create` takes `parent_id`, and there is a `move` action; `browse`, `search_assets` and `browse_map` take `include_descendants`; `list` rows carry the path, depth and subtree count.
- **`search_assets`** takes `name_contains` (the Find by List filter) and `has_raw`. Smart-collection rules accept both.

## Fixed

- The Tags page and the folder page no longer show an empty inspector on the right; their content uses the full width.
- With no nested folders, the folder list lines up with the smart collections above it.
- The nightly coverage run tests the Electron suite again. No change to the app.
