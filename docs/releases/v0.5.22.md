AfterFrame 0.5.22 gives the photo more of the screen. In the lightbox it now fills the window, and the bars come out only when you reach for them, or stay out with a pin. A catalog on a network drive imports faster, and AfterFrame warns you before you put one there.

## Download

| | File | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| **Mac, Apple Silicon** | `AfterFrame-0.5.22-arm64.dmg` | macOS 14 or later |
| **Mac, Apple Silicon, older macOS** | `AfterFrame-0.5.22-macOS12-arm64.dmg` | macOS 12 or 13 |
| **Mac, Intel** | `AfterFrame-0.5.22-macOS12-Intel.dmg` | macOS 12 or later |
| **Windows** | `AfterFrame-0.5.22-x64-setup.exe` | Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit |

The two macOS 12 builds are the same app without People, Scene Depth and Stickers, which use Apple's on-device models that need macOS 14. On an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 14 or later, take the first one. The Intel build reads RAW files with an older LibRaw (0.21), which can't decode DNGs compressed with JPEG XL.

The Windows installer isn't signed yet. When Windows says "Windows protected your PC" (Windows 已保护你的电脑), click **More info** (更多信息), then **Run anyway** (仍要运行). To update, run the new installer: your catalogs and settings stay. LUTs, Stickers, People and Scene Depth are in the macOS app only for now.

A catalog opened by 0.5.19 or later can't be opened by 0.5.18 or older (the folder tree and tag groups added in 0.5.19). 0.5.22 doesn't change the catalog format.

## New

### A lightbox that gets out of the way

The lightbox used to keep its header, zoom row and filmstrip on screen all the time, and fit the photo in the space between them.

- **The photo fills the window.** The header, the zoom row with the filmstrip and the arrows are hidden until the pointer reaches the top, the bottom or an arrow, and fade out again once it is back on the photo. The arrow keys page either way.
- **The pin keeps them out.** The new pin button, between Info and Close, brings back the layout from before: bars always showing, photo fitted between them. AfterFrame remembers your choice.
- **The details** (**I**) float over the photo when the bars are unpinned, and make room for it when they are pinned.

Videos and Proof keep their usual layout. Thanks to @mahuaii for this one (#290).

## Changed

### Catalogs on a network drive

SQLite, which holds the catalog, is slow on a network share: every save takes several round trips to the server, and a large import can stall.

- **A warning** comes up when you create or open a catalog on a network volume (an SMB, AFP or NFS share on the Mac, a mapped drive or a `\\server\share` path on Windows). You can choose another location or go ahead anyway.
- **Imports save less often.** Progress is written at most twice a second, and finished previews are saved together. 40 DNGs from a NAS now import in about 40 seconds instead of 51. A catalog on the Mac is still much faster: under a second for the same files.

### Also in this release

- **Arrow keys in the waterfall layout wrap at the edges**, like a line of text: Right in the last column goes on to the first column one line down, and Left in the first column goes back up. They used to stop there.
- **Dragging a folder from Finder over the sidebar's Folders section** now outlines the whole section, which is where the drop lands, instead of just its heading.
