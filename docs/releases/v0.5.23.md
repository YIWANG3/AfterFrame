AfterFrame 0.5.23 adds a Statistics page: what you shot with, how you shoot, how far the culling has got, where and when you shoot, and where the disk space goes. Most of its numbers open the photos behind them in the gallery.

## Download

| | File | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| **Mac, Apple Silicon** | `AfterFrame-0.5.23-arm64.dmg` | macOS 14 or later |
| **Mac, Apple Silicon, older macOS** | `AfterFrame-0.5.23-macOS12-arm64.dmg` | macOS 12 or 13 |
| **Mac, Intel** | `AfterFrame-0.5.23-macOS12-Intel.dmg` | macOS 12 or later |
| **Windows** | `AfterFrame-0.5.23-x64-setup.exe` | Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit |

The two macOS 12 builds are the same app without People, Scene Depth and Stickers, which use Apple's on-device models that need macOS 14. On an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 14 or later, take the first one. The Intel build reads RAW files with an older LibRaw (0.21), which can't decode DNGs compressed with JPEG XL.

The Windows installer isn't signed yet. When Windows says "Windows protected your PC" (Windows 已保护你的电脑), click **More info** (更多信息), then **Run anyway** (仍要运行). To update, run the new installer: your catalogs and settings stay. LUTs, Stickers, People and Scene Depth are in the macOS app only for now.

A catalog opened by 0.5.19 or later can't be opened by 0.5.18 or older (the folder tree and tag groups added in 0.5.19). 0.5.23 doesn't change the catalog format.

## New

### Statistics

**Statistics** in the sidebar, under Tags, opens a page about your library for **All time**, **This year** or the **Last 12 months**. It reads what the catalog already holds, so there's nothing to scan first.

- **Overview**: photos and videos, shooting days, the span of capture dates, keep rate (picks among everything flagged), countries and cities, and the space it all takes.
- **Gear**: each camera with its count, keep rate, most-used lens and a 24-month strip of when you used it, marked once it has sat unused for six months; lenses; and the body and lens pairs you shoot most. Camera models read the way people say them (`ILCE-7CM2` is a Sony A7C II).
- **How you shoot**: focal length in 35 mm terms (phones and drones apart), aperture, shutter speed with the reject rate at each stop, ISO, orientation and file types.
- **Culling**: imported, looked at, picked, rated 4★ or more and edited, what you haven't looked at yet, and how the ratings spread.
- **Places**: the cities you've shot in most.
- **When you shoot**: a calendar of shooting days for each year, photos per month, per weekday and per hour.
- **Storage**: space by file type and by year, and what could go: rejected photos, look-alikes beyond the best one of each group, and files missing from disk. A RAW shared by several versions is counted once, and only when every version using it would go. Nothing is deleted from this page.

Click a camera, a lens, a bar or a number and the gallery opens with that filter and the page's time range. Shutter speed and 35 mm focal length show their numbers on hover only, since the filter bar has no filter for them. The page refreshes by itself after an import or a round of culling.
