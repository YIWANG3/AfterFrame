AfterFrame 0.5.12 plays videos on the Mac from their original files at full resolution, ProRes included, and imports what you drop on its Dock icon.

## Download

| | File | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| **Mac, Apple Silicon** | `AfterFrame-0.5.12-arm64.dmg` | macOS 14 or later |
| **Mac, Apple Silicon, older macOS** | `AfterFrame-0.5.12-macOS12-arm64.dmg` | macOS 12 or 13 |
| **Mac, Intel** | `AfterFrame-0.5.12-macOS12-Intel.dmg` | macOS 12 or later |
| **Windows** | `AfterFrame-0.5.12-x64-setup.exe` | Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit |

The two macOS 12 builds are the same app without People, Scene Depth and Stickers, which use Apple's on-device models that need macOS 14. On an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 14 or later, take the first one. The Intel build reads RAW files with an older LibRaw (0.21), which can't decode DNGs compressed with JPEG XL.

The Windows installer isn't signed yet. When Windows says "Windows protected your PC" (Windows 已保护你的电脑), click **More info** (更多信息), then **Run anyway** (仍要运行). To update, run the new installer: your catalogs and settings stay. LUTs, Stickers, People and Scene Depth are in the macOS app only for now.

## Highlights

### Videos play from the original on the Mac

- The lightbox plays a video with AVPlayer, macOS's own player, straight from the original file and at its full resolution. Nothing is transcoded first, so there's no wait for a playback copy.
- **ProRes** clips play. Before, they played sound with no picture while a 1080p copy was made to play instead.
- The controls are the ones you know: play and pause, the scrubber, volume, mute and the space bar.
- **Fullscreen** stays on when you step to the next clip, and closing the lightbox puts the window back the way it was.
- If macOS can't play a clip, the lightbox falls back to the player it used before, with its 1080p copy.

Windows plays videos as in 0.5.11.

### Import from the Dock

Drop photos, RAW files, videos or folders on AfterFrame's icon in the Dock (程序坞) to import them, whether the app is open or not. Double-clicking these files still opens them in the app they opened in before: AfterFrame doesn't take over any file type.

### Also in this release

- **Settings looks as it should again.** Since 0.5.10 the Settings window had lost its rounder corners and its filled, borderless fields and buttons, and dragging its title row no longer moved the window.
- **A RAW opens in the editor on its HD preview.** When the last editing session ended in the LUT tool, the next RAW you opened could open on Apple's rendering of the RAW instead, or switch to it half a second later. Apple's rendering looks different from the camera's own JPEG, and is meant only for grading with a LUT. Now the editor always opens on Crop.
