AfterFrame now runs on Windows. This release also adds Show in All Assets, makes a cleared rating stay cleared, and keeps the app quicker to answer while an import runs.

## Download

| | File | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| **Mac** | `AfterFrame-0.5.10-arm64.dmg` | macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon |
| **Windows** | `AfterFrame-0.5.10-x64-setup.exe` | Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit |

## Windows

### Installing

The Windows installer isn't signed yet, so Windows warns the first time you run it:

1. "Windows protected your PC" (Windows 已保护你的电脑): click **More info** (更多信息).
2. Click **Run anyway** (仍要运行).

If your browser says the file isn't commonly downloaded, choose to keep it.

The installer asks whether to install for you or for everyone on the PC, and where. To update later, download the new installer and run it. Your catalogs and settings stay.

### What the Mac version has that Windows doesn't yet

- **Stickers, People and Scene Depth** (text behind the subject) use Apple's on-device models. On Windows they say "Available in the macOS app for now": Stickers and People in the sidebar, sticker layers and Scene Depth in the editor.
- **Videos:** H.264 videos play, but newly imported videos have no cover frame or duration. HEVC (H.265) videos, the iPhone's default, play black, because Windows has no HEVC decoder by default.
- **Open With** doesn't list other editors.
- **DNGs compressed with JPEG XL** (Adobe DNG Converter's lossy option, some Lightroom and iPhone DNGs) can't be fully decoded. They browse fine, but the editor uses their embedded preview, which can be small.

Everything else works as on the Mac: importing and watched folders, HEIC and RAW, the editor, frames, collages, Split, the map, smart collections and search. Chinese file names and folders are fine, and no graphics card is needed.

## Highlights

### Show in All Assets

From a search, a filter, a folder, a smart collection, a person or another view, right-click a photo and choose **Show in All Assets** (在全部素材中定位). The search and filters clear, your sort stays, and the photo is selected in the middle of the grid with the photos taken around it.

### Ratings

- **Pressing 0 clears the stars** in the Inspector too. Before, a photo whose file carries stars (from the camera or a Lightroom export) showed them again after you cleared them.
- **A cleared rating stays cleared.** Before, importing or refreshing the photo again brought back the file's stars.
- **Stars clicked in the Inspector** no longer stop the number keys from changing them.

### Imports

- **Folders chosen while another import runs** are imported after it. Before, a folder picked just as an import ended could be dropped without a word.
- **The app answers sooner during an import.** Imports and other background jobs run at a lower priority, so the gallery and the sidebar come first. An import on an otherwise idle computer takes as long as before.
- **Place names use less memory and come sooner.** Each background process loads a small place index instead of the whole place list: about 370 MB less memory, and the first place name in about 0.05 s instead of 0.6 s on a Mac.
- **RAW, TIFF and video cards waiting for their thumbnails** say "Preparing preview…" during an import. Before, they tried to repair themselves, which could hold up the whole app for seconds at a time on a hard drive. Thumbnails still missing afterwards, for example after a cancelled import, are made in the background.
- **Watched folders:** sending a photo by AirDrop, tagging it in Finder or opening it in another app no longer starts an import. Only new or changed files do.
