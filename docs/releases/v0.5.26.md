AfterFrame 0.5.26 lets you scale the whole interface, so a big screen shows more at once or a small one reads more easily. The Similar strip in the Inspector now lists only photos of the same scene, and the lightbox's details pane is easier to read over a photo.

## Download

| | File | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| **Mac, Apple Silicon** | `AfterFrame-0.5.26-arm64.dmg` | macOS 14 or later |
| **Mac, Apple Silicon, older macOS** | `AfterFrame-0.5.26-macOS12-arm64.dmg` | macOS 12 or 13 |
| **Mac, Intel** | `AfterFrame-0.5.26-macOS12-Intel.dmg` | macOS 12 or later |
| **Windows** | `AfterFrame-0.5.26-x64-setup.exe` | Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit |

The two macOS 12 builds are the same app without People, Scene Depth and Stickers, which use Apple's on-device models that need macOS 14. On an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 14 or later, take the first one. The Intel build reads RAW files with an older LibRaw (0.21), which can't decode DNGs compressed with JPEG XL.

The Windows installer isn't signed yet. When Windows says "Windows protected your PC" (Windows 已保护你的电脑), click **More info** (更多信息), then **Run anyway** (仍要运行). To update, run the new installer: your catalogs and settings stay. LUTs, Stickers, People and Scene Depth are in the macOS app only for now.

A catalog opened by 0.5.19 or later can't be opened by 0.5.18 or older (the folder tree and tag groups added in 0.5.19). 0.5.26 doesn't change the catalog format.

## New

### Interface scale

- **Settings → General → Interface scale** scales the whole window to 80, 90, 100, 110 or 125 %. It takes effect at once and stays after a restart. The default is still 100 %, so nothing changes until you pick a scale.
- **View → Zoom In / Zoom Out / Actual Size** on ⌘= / ⌘- / ⌘0 (Ctrl+= / Ctrl+- / Ctrl+0 on Windows) step one notch up or down, or go back to 100 %. The number pad's + / - / 0 work too, and you can rebind all three in Settings → Shortcuts. The thumbnail-size keys stay plain `=` and `-` (#309).

The scale isn't part of a settings backup yet.

## Changed

### The Similar strip shows only the same scene

The **Similar** strip in the Inspector (and in the lightbox's details pane) used to show the eight nearest photos however far off they were. A photo with no real look-alike got eight photos of the same kind of subject from other days. It now lists only photos of the same scene: another frame of a burst, the same spot recomposed, another crop or edit of the picture. So it often shows fewer than eight, and for a photo with nothing close it isn't shown at all.

**Find Similar** (the full gallery view) and the MCP `find_similar` tool are unchanged and still give the full ranked list. No re-indexing needed (#308).

## Fixed

- **The lightbox's details pane is readable over a photo.** It has a dark, blurred ground of its own instead of a nearly transparent one, and the seam that showed on the photo at the pane's left edge is gone (#307).
- **The tag picker stays open when a short window puts Add tag at the bottom edge.** Adding a photo's first tag of a group pushed the input out of view and the picker closed itself. It now scrolls the input back into view (#308).
