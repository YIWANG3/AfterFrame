AfterFrame 0.5.25 is a small fix release: switching to another app while you type a description or a name no longer saves the half-typed text, and two labels in the collage panel and the lightbox now say the right thing.

## Download

| | File | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| **Mac, Apple Silicon** | `AfterFrame-0.5.25-arm64.dmg` | macOS 14 or later |
| **Mac, Apple Silicon, older macOS** | `AfterFrame-0.5.25-macOS12-arm64.dmg` | macOS 12 or 13 |
| **Mac, Intel** | `AfterFrame-0.5.25-macOS12-Intel.dmg` | macOS 12 or later |
| **Windows** | `AfterFrame-0.5.25-x64-setup.exe` | Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit |

The two macOS 12 builds are the same app without People, Scene Depth and Stickers, which use Apple's on-device models that need macOS 14. On an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 14 or later, take the first one. The Intel build reads RAW files with an older LibRaw (0.21), which can't decode DNGs compressed with JPEG XL.

The Windows installer isn't signed yet. When Windows says "Windows protected your PC" (Windows 已保护你的电脑), click **More info** (更多信息), then **Run anyway** (仍要运行). To update, run the new installer: your catalogs and settings stay. LUTs, Stickers, People and Scene Depth are in the macOS app only for now.

A catalog opened by 0.5.19 or later can't be opened by 0.5.18 or older (the folder tree and tag groups added in 0.5.19). 0.5.25 doesn't change the catalog format.

## Changed

### Switching apps keeps your draft

A photo's description in the Inspector, and a name typed in place (a new folder, a rename, a smart collection, a watermark, logo or border name), used to be saved and closed as soon as the AfterFrame window lost focus. Switching to another app mid-sentence saved the half-typed text.

- **Leaving the window** now keeps the field open with its draft and saves nothing. When you come back, the cursor is back in the field and you can keep typing.
- **Inside the app** nothing changes: clicking elsewhere or pressing Tab saves, Enter saves, Escape discards, and moving to another photo drops an open description draft (#302).

## Fixed

- **The lightbox gives an upright photo's size the right way round.** For a portrait photo stored as landscape pixels (a camera held upright usually stores it that way), the lightbox header said 7040 × 4688 while the grid and the Inspector said 4688 × 7040. It now matches them (#304).
- **The collage cell panel's Center and Reset buttons** follow the app language. In Chinese they read 居中 and 重置 instead of staying in English (#303).
