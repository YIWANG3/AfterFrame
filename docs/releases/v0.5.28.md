AfterFrame 0.5.28 keeps the albums you make apart from the folders on disk. What the app called folders are now **collections**, and a new **Folders** section mirrors the folders your photos are in, without ever changing them. It recognizes cats and dogs beside people. The Frame tool gains backgrounds made from the photo, and camera mockups that show the photo on a camera's own screen.

The two macOS 12 builds are the same app without People & Pets, Scene Depth and Stickers, which use Apple's on-device models that need macOS 14. On an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 14 or later, take the first one. The Intel build reads RAW files with an older LibRaw (0.21), which can't decode DNGs compressed with JPEG XL.

The Windows installer isn't signed yet. When Windows says "Windows protected your PC" (Windows 已保护你的电脑), click **More info** (更多信息), then **Run anyway** (仍要运行). To update, run the new installer: your catalogs and settings stay. LUTs, Stickers, People & Pets and Scene Depth are in the macOS app only for now.

**This version upgrades your catalog.** The first time 0.5.28 opens a catalog it makes room for pets, and 0.5.27 or older can't open that catalog afterwards. A copy of the catalog as it was is left next to it (`catalog.sqlite3.schema15.bak`), should you need to go back.

## New

### Collections, and the folders on disk

- **Folders are now collections** (合集). The albums you make and fill yourself are called collections everywhere: the sidebar, the menus, the Collections page, Settings and the agent tools' descriptions. Nothing in them changes: the same photos, the same nesting, the same order.
- **Folders** is a new section in the sidebar, under Collections, that mirrors the folders on disk your photos are in, drive by drive. A chain of folders that each lead to only one other folds into one row.
  - Click a folder to see its photos, with or without the folders inside it. The Inspector's Folder row opens a photo's folder.
  - Right-click a folder for **Locate…** (when it was moved or renamed in Finder), **Sync**, **Sync Automatically**, **Save as Collection…**, **Show in Finder**, and **Remove from Library…** or Move to Trash.
  - **Hide This Level**, on a top folder that holds no photos itself, moves the folders in it up to the top. A folder moved up this way has **Show the Level Above** to bring it back. This Mac remembers what you hid (#339).
  - Nothing here makes, renames or moves a folder on disk, and collections never follow folders. **Save as Collection…** takes a one-time copy of a folder's photos.
  - The catalog's own folder, where the app writes your crops, text and frames, is left out of the tree. Those versions stay in All Assets and in their stacks (#339).
- **Synced folders.** Sync Automatically is the watched folder of old, and Settings → Library now calls the list **Synced folders**. A synced folder now also notices files and folders that leave it or are moved in whole, and marks their photos missing at once instead of at the next Verify. A folder inside a synced one shows the sync mark too, and hovering the mark says which folder it syncs with (#333).
- **Smart collections can be searched in the sidebar**, as collections and folders already could (#333).

For agents, MCP `search_assets` takes `folder`, and `maintain_library` gains `list_folders` and `locate_folder`. Parameter names that say folder for a collection are unchanged (#319).

### A moved photo keeps its record

Move or rename an original in Finder and import it again, or let a synced folder or the next launch find it: the app now links the photo you already had to the file's new place, with its rating, tags, collections and versions, instead of adding a second photo. It does so when the file's content and type match a photo whose own file is gone. A copy, with the old file still there, is still a new photo. The import toast says "Found 3 moved files", and those are no longer counted as already in the catalog. **Relink All from Folder…** in Missing Originals now brings a photo's paired RAW along (#319).

### People & Pets

- **Cats and dogs are recognized beside people**, and each animal's photos form a group of its own on the same wall. Turn it on in Settings → People & Pets → **Recognize pets**; it is off by default. A library already analyzed for faces is offered **Analyze pets** once, and only the pets are worked out. Turned off again, the pets already found stay.
- **The wall** has an All / People / Pets switch beside Scan, pet tiles carry a paw, and a pet's cover is cropped to its head. Naming and merging offer only the same species, so a cat and a dog never share a group.
- **The filter bar** has a Pets chip (Pets → Cats → Dogs → off), and the Inspector lists a photo's pets with their species.
- **Only cats and dogs.** Vision's animal finder knows nothing else, so anything animal-shaped used to come back as a cat or a dog. Each find now gets a second look with some of the scene around it, and a cow, a deer, a seal, a rock or a plush toy is dropped. A black-and-white cow framed very tightly can still pass for a dalmatian (#330).
- Like faces, it all runs on the Mac. The pet model (DINOv2 Small) ships in the Apple Silicon macOS 14 app, which grows by about 40 MB.
- "People" is now "People & Pets" (人物与宠物) wherever it shows, and "Scan faces" is "Scan" (#325).
- **The sample library has a cat**, Mimi. A new sample library has 15 photos; one you already have keeps its 14 until you reset it (#326).

For agents, MCP `list_people` returns `subject` and filters by it, `index_people` takes `include_pets`, and `search_assets` takes `pets`.

### Frame backgrounds made from the photo

- **Photo colours:** a gradient from the photo's own top and bottom colours, worked out for each photo. **Blur:** the photo itself, scaled to cover the canvas and blurred, with a Blur slider (#320).
- **Ratio:** Photo, 1:1, 4:5, 3:4 or 9:16. The ratio adds margin on the narrow side; the photo stays centred and is never cropped.
- **Corners** and **Shadow** sliders for the photo, and a **Corners** slider for a text layer's background.
- Picking Photo colours or Blur on a photo with no margin gives it an 8 % margin, corners and a shadow; settings you already made are kept.
- Changing the background or applying a template recolours frame text and plain camera logos in the margin that would no longer read, such as dark ink on a dark blur. Text and logos on the photo, and brand colours such as Canon red, are left alone.
- **Four presets** use them: a blurred card, a photo-colours card, a blurred 9:16 story and a photo-colours 4:5 (#334, #337).
- Saved templates now keep the ratio, the background, the corners and the shadow. Templates saved before keep working as they did.

For agents, `get_editor_capabilities` lists the four presets (family `photo-bg`), and `apply_frame` renders them.

### Camera mockups

- **Two new frame templates** show the photo on the screen of a camera seen from the back: the camera alone, or the camera above the photo. Both have a version without the line of text under the camera (#335).
- **75 cameras.** A photo gets its own camera by its EXIF model, else its maker's usual camera, else a generic one: a Hasselblad 907X for a landscape photo, a Leica M11-P held upright for a portrait one (#338).
- A **Camera** section in the Frame panel picks another camera, holds it level or upright, and sets the screen's corners, the camera picture's exposure and a reflection on the glass.
- Under the camera, its name and the exposure. Under a camera of another maker (an iPhone's photo on the generic Leica) the name is left out.
- A saved template keeps its camera, or "the photo's own camera" when that's what it showed.

For agents, `apply_frame` takes `camera-screen` like any other template.

## Changed

- **The lightbox pin no longer moves or resizes the photo.** Pinned bars now lie over the photo's top and bottom, as revealed bars do when unpinned, and a zoomed photo goes on under the bars and the details pane either way. This replaces the 0.5.22 rule that a pinned photo is fitted between the bars (#331).
- **The lightbox's controls fade in** over 160 ms and wait half a second before fading out when the pointer comes back onto the photo. Hidden controls give up keyboard focus, so Tab skips them, and the fades follow the system's Reduce Motion setting (#321).
- **Switching frame templates drops what the last one set.** A ratio, corners or shadow that a template set and you haven't touched since goes when the next template says nothing about them; one you set yourself stays. A ratio picked over a template lays the template out again, so its text stays on the canvas (#334, #335).
- **The 留白 · 仅机型 template** stacks its model and exposure lines as a group centred in the margin (#337).
- **Sony's α in frame text** reads "a7C II" in the bundled frame fonts, which have no α; before, that one letter fell back to a thinner system font. A font that has α keeps it, and the camera's name elsewhere is unchanged (#336).
- **Analyzing an edited photo's faces again keeps what you decided.** The new face in the old one's place keeps its person, its hidden group and its "not this person". Before, re-analysis dropped the photo's faces from every group (#329).
- **A collection that holds only other collections shows their photos** on its card on the Collections page: count, cover, capture span and last-added time, and so in the sort and the filters there too. It used to show as empty (#327).
- **The sidebar's Rated count follows a rating at once.** It used to catch up only at the next click in the sidebar or the next import (#328).
- **A short sidebar section keeps its heading and first three rows** when a long one is beside it. Three smart collections next to forty collections with covers used to keep only their heading (#333).

## Fixed

- **The sidebar's Recently Added, Rated and Look-alikes counts match the grid** when RAW+JPG pairs are merged. Each pair used to count twice there (#327).
- **The glass under the lightbox's bars fades out at any interface scale** instead of ending in a hard line across the photo (#323, #340).
- **The lightbox title keeps clear of the traffic lights** at every interface scale. At 80 % the file name ran into the green light. In full screen, where there are no lights, it starts at the left edge (#332).
- **A photo's description grows while you write it**, in the Inspector and the lightbox, and opens showing its full text (#322).
- **The agent's messages in the app follow its language.** "Selected 3 photos", "Collections updated." and the others were in English in 中文 (#327).
