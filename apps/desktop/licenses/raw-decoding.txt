RAW decoding — third-party licenses
===================================

The sidecar reads RAW files (embedded previews, size and orientation, and
decodes) with LibRaw, through rawpy. Both ship as separate libraries inside
the sidecar (Resources/sidecar/media-workspace): LibRaw as its own shared
library (libraw_r), which can be replaced with a compatible build.
