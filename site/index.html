All your photos,
in one place.

Bring photos from different folders and drives into one catalog to browse, filter, rate, tag, and collect. The originals stay where they are.

Titles no font
could give you.

04 / Collage

Arrange a shoot

Choose a layout, pan and zoom each cell, adjust gaps, padding, corners, and background, then export at high resolution.

06 / MCP

Ask for the photos
you mean.

With the local MCP server, an agent can search, tag, crop, export, and show selections in the app.

Connect an agent →

Find every photo
of the same person.
